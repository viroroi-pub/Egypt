----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Overview
----------------------------------------------------------------

This software provides an interactive 3D visualization of the
Integrated Edge-Ramp (IER) construction model proposed for the
Khufu and Khafre Pyramids of Giza.

The IER model is a construction hypothesis in which narrow,
edge-integrated helicoidal ramps are progressively embedded
along the pyramid’s corners. This approach minimizes external
ramp material, preserves geometric precision, and enables
efficient logistics throughout the full construction sequence.

The visualization integrates the pyramid with its surrounding
paleo-environment, including the Ahramat branch of the Nile and
associated harbor infrastructure, emphasizing fluvial logistics
and landscape context.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. Purpose
----------------------------------------------------------------

This executable is intended as:

- A research visualization supporting the associated preprint
  and forthcoming journal publication.
- An educational and outreach tool for understanding pyramid
  construction processes.
- A spatial and geometric companion to the computational and
  analytical framework presented in the paper.

The software is not a game and contains no scoring or objectives.

----------------------------------------------------------------
3. Key Features
----------------------------------------------------------------

- Interactive 3D reconstruction of the Great Pyramid
- Visualization of edge-integrated helicoidal ramps starting
  at the pyramid corners
- Phased construction logic
- Landscape context including Nile channel and harbor area
- Standalone executable (no installation required)

----------------------------------------------------------------
4. Controls
----------------------------------------------------------------

Mouse:
- Left click + drag: rotate camera
- Right click + drag / scroll: zoom
- Middle click + drag: pan

Keyboard (if enabled):
- W / A / S / D: move camera
- Q / E: vertical movement
- Esc: exit application
